## 1.0

First Apps Catalog release.

- MCW (FM tone), CW (OOK) and SSB (USB/LSB) keying modes
- Repeater-style activity trigger, fixed-interval trigger, or both, with
  start-of-TX / end-of-TX interval anchoring
- Remote text over BLE serial or UART (pins 13/14)
- PARIS-standard timing with ARRL Farnsworth spacing
- Station ID log, capped at 256 KB with rotation
